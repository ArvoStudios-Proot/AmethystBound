Amethyst Bound Wiki

Welcome to the official community wiki for Amethyst Bound. This mod bridges the gap between Iron and Diamond tiers by introducing a complete suite of crystal-infused gear, weapons, armor sets, and mount barding.

Mod Requirements

Amethyst Bound requires the Fabric API to be installed in your client and server mods folder for Minecraft versions 26.1.2 through 26.3.

Crafting & Recipes

All tools and armor follow intuitive vanilla crafting shapes using Amethyst Shards and sticks.
