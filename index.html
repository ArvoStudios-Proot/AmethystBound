Amethyst Bound

Elevate your Minecraft progression by turning abundant Amethyst Shards into a reliable mid-tier gear set that bridges the exact gap between Iron and Diamond.

Before installing Amethyst Bound, ensure your instance meets the following loader and dependency prerequisites:

Fabric API (Required)

Mandatory core dependency. Place in your /mods folder.

Fully tested and optimized for Fabric loader on current MC versions.

Crafting Recipes & Patterns

All Amethyst tools and armor pieces utilize familiar vanilla crafting table layouts, replacing iron ingots or diamonds directly with Amethyst Shards.

Crafting Note

Vanilla Patterns + Amethyst Dagger

Standard tools and armor pieces use exact vanilla shapes. The Amethyst Dagger crafts cleanly with 1 Amethyst Shard above 1 Stick.
