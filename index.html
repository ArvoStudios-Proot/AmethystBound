Overview

wiki/overview

Amethyst Bound is a simple, vanilla-friendly extension mod that gives you even more uses for Amethyst! To make things vanilla-friendly, tools and armor keep the same classic vanilla look, and the crafting recipes are exactly the same as standard vanilla gear.

Whether you are mining deep underground or exploring oceans with your Nautilus, Amethyst gear provides a balanced progression step right between Iron and Diamond.

Required Dependency: Fabric API

You must have Fabric API installed in your /mods folder. Without this, the mod will not work!

Tools & Amethyst Dagger

wiki/tools

All Amethyst tools keep the vanilla look and craft with the exact same recipes as standard tools—plus the new Amethyst Dagger for players who prefer smaller, compact swords!

Crafting Note

Vanilla Patterns + Amethyst Dagger

Standard tools (Sword, Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe) and Armor pieces use exact vanilla shapes. The Amethyst Dagger crafts cleanly with 1 Amethyst Shard above 1 Stick.

What It Doesn't Include

  • ✕ No special items besides the Amethyst Dagger... yet.
  • ✕ Spear: Had multiple issues trying to implement this—will try to fix and add it later!
  • ✕ Armor Trims: Compatibility with Smithing Table armor trims is not supported yet.
  • ✕ Lights, Blocks, & Other Items: Not in the current build (I'm not spoiling!).
